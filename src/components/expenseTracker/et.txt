12. LOCAL STORAGE
-----------------
Save expenses in localStorage.

Save:

- Expenses
- Income
- Budget
- Categories
- Settings

Example:

localStorage.setItem(
    "expenses",
    JSON.stringify(expenses)
);

Load:

const savedExpenses =
    localStorage.getItem("expenses");

const expenses =
    savedExpenses
        ? JSON.parse(savedExpenses)
        : [];
